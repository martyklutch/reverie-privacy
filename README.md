Privacy Policy for Reverie Journal

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Overview
Reverie Journal ("the app") is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data Collection
We do not collect any personal information. All journal entries, photos, and settings are stored privately on your device and in your personal iCloud account. No data is transmitted to us or any third party.

iCloud
If you use iCloud, your journal data syncs privately through Apple's iCloud service between your own devices. This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and is never accessible to us.

In-App Purchases
Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

Notifications
If you enable daily reminders, these are local notifications generated on your device. No data leaves your device for this feature.
